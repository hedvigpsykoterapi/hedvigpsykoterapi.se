# Generated wood-border concepts

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original PNGs and optimized WebP copies are in `assets/border-concepts/`.

## botanical

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: sophisticated horizontal wooden border for a wine-red psychotherapy website. Generate ONE wide landscape image, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. Entire composition is a single horizontal band of Dense hand-carved floral scrollwork inspired by antique Indian walnut room dividers: asymmetrical curling acanthus tendrils, deeply veined leaves, small nested flowers, intricate interconnecting foliage, open pierced gaps. Elegant fine craftsmanship, no simple repeated cartoon flowers. Straight-on orthographic product photograph, zero perspective, finely polished dark chocolate walnut with visible natural grain and reddish-brown highlights, realistic bevels, carved depth and soft self-shadow. Cutout holes show flat warm ivory #f7f2ea behind the carving. The ornament fills the canvas from left to right and nearly top to bottom, with thin continuous wood rails along top and bottom. No wall scene, no floor, no text, no labels, no watermark, no collage. Keep ornament readable when reduced to a narrow web divider, but much more sophisticated than a vector icon. Calm elegant artisan character.
